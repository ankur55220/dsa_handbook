# Week 1 Discoveries

## Discovery 1 - Output Classification

Every problem first asks:
What is being optimized?

Examples:
- Minimize
- Maximize
- Count
- Enumerate
- Decide

---

## Discovery 2 - Control Engine

The optimization objective naturally determines the traversal.

Examples:

Minimum Unweighted

↓

BFS

Minimum Weighted

↓

Dijkstra

Exhaustive Search

↓

DFS

Overlapping Subproblems

↓

DP

---

## Discovery 3 - Algorithm Derivation

Don't ask:

"What algorithm is this?"

Ask:

"What optimization am I solving?"

The algorithm follows naturally.
